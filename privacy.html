Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

Smart Finance Bot is a personal-use app operated by xinjie. It has one user: its developer.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app uses the Google Sheets scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets) to read and write the owner's own bookkeeping spreadsheet.

How data is used and stored

Spreadsheet data is used only to record and summarize the owner's transactions. The app does not store a separate copy; data stays in the owner's Google account. Transaction text the owner sends is processed by an AI model service and a messaging service solely to provide this function.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account's third-party access settings. Contact: limxj0208@gmail.com
